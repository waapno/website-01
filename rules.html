§1

Chapter 1

Account, characters & items

Prohibited

04

Account sharing

Sharing game accounts, especially login credentials, is prohibited.

ExceptionSharing an account for short-term help, such as buffs or minor assistance, is tolerated as long as nobody profits from it.
05

Account sharing for profit

Lending or taking over game accounts to gain advantages a player would not normally get is not allowed (e.g. moving earned in-game currency from a strong player's account to a weaker one).

06

Circumventing fair play

Any attempt to speed up in-game progress through someone else's account that the player does not own.

Penalty
  1. Temporary ban
  2. Permanent ban
07

Inappropriate names

A character may not carry a name protected by trademark or copyright, the name of a real public figure, a name used as advertising, or contain swear words or offensive terms. This also applies to guild names, pets and other in-game entities.

Penalty
  1. Warning
  2. Temporary ban
  3. Permanent ban

Chapter 2

Fair play & game integrity

Prohibited

01

Bug abuse

Exploiting bugs.

02

Bypassing game systems

Any attempt to bypass game settings, systems or the core principles of the game.

Penalty
  1. Temporary ban
  2. Permanent ban
Players must report all bugs immediately.
03

Botting & automation

Using any programs, scripts, macros or other external tools that make the game easier or automate it in any way.

Using methods that perform in-game actions without the player's active involvement.

Penalty
  1. Permanent ban of all accounts
Players must stay actively engaged while playing and keep track of what is happening in the game. Using methods that let a player perform repetitive actions without watching the game screen may be considered botting.
04

Unethical trading practices

Any attempt at pressure, threats or manipulation when trading between players (including the market, private trades, etc.). This includes (but is not limited to):

  • Threatening in-game violence over the prices of traded items.
  • Pressuring players to change a price under threat of attacking their character or guild.
  • Organised market manipulation to control prices.
  • Forcing trades where the player has no choice.
  • Exploiting players' inattention with incorrectly entered prices.
Penalty
  1. Temporary ban
  2. Permanent ban

§4

Chapter 4

Hard Grief — targeted player killing

Hard Grief is prohibited

This rule applies exclusively to the intentional, targeted killing of other players in order to ruin their game.

HG

Hard Grief includes in particular

  • Targeted killing of players without PvM gear.
  • Using pure PvP characters to repeatedly kill players.
  • Deliberately using only “PvP weapons” to hunt and repeatedly kill weaker players.
  • Hard Hunt – deliberately seeking out and killing much weaker players on maps meant for lower levels. For example, a level 200 player repeatedly hunting players on a level 192 map purely to kill them and disrupt their game.
Simply being on a lower-level map, owning PvP gear or a single PvP fight is not automatically a violation. What matters is a provable intent to deliberately kill players and disrupt their game.

Review & evidence

When investigating suspected Hard Grief, an administrator may consider available game logs, the circumstances of fights, character gear and other relevant evidence. Where the game system allows it, the suspect's inventory may also be checked.

Penalties

  1. Warning
  2. Temporary
  3. Permanent

Permanent ban for repeated or serious violations. The administration decides the penalty based on the severity of the conduct, available evidence and any repetition.

§

Server administration

Rule enforcement

The server admin team is responsible for enforcing all rules and keeping the game fair. Admins have the right to monitor player behaviour, assess rule violations and take appropriate action.

Depending on the severity of the violation, a ban may include chat restrictions, an account ban, a ban of all accounts, or a ban of all accounts of every player involved.
+

For staff members

Rules addendum for administrators

  1. 01Staff members must not humiliate, insult or intimidate anyone.
  2. 02During their shifts, staff members must watch players for rule violations and help them with questions.
  3. 03Staff members are strictly forbidden from fighting players.
  4. 04Staff members are strictly forbidden from using inappropriate or vulgar language.
  5. 05Staff members must spend most of their time in invisible mode. Events are the exception.
  6. 06Staff members must respond to situations (player checks, questions) within a reasonable time.
  7. 07Staff members may use communication channels (public game chat, announcements) only when unavoidable.
  8. 08Staff members keep contact with players to a minimum.
  9. 09Staff members may not create events without the owner's permission.
  10. 10Staff members may own game accounts but must not reveal their identity (or their colleagues') to other players. If discovered, the situation will be discussed with the owner.
  11. 11Staff members must not abuse their powers. In such a case is team member instantly kicked out.
  12. 12Staff members must not favour or disadvantage players.
  13. 13Staff members are not entitled to any rewards and work for the server entirely voluntarily.

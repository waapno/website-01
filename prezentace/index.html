FACTIONS

The core of the server. Build a 3-man squad, dig in, secure your claim and hold your spot in the Faction Top. Alliances are allowed – but remember, too many friends means too many enemies.

CommandWhat it does
/f create <name>Creates a new faction.
/f invite <player>Invites a player (max 3 members).
/f join <name>Join a faction after an invite.
/f claimClaims the chunk you stand on.
/f unclaimReleases a claimed chunk.
/f mapShows the claim map around you.
/f homeTeleports to your faction base.
/f sethomeSets the faction home.
/f ally <faction>Offers an alliance.
/f enemy <faction>Declares war.
/f topFaction leaderboard – decides the payout.
/f who <faction>Faction info, power and claims.

CLASSES

4–5 classes with unique passives and utilities. Your class depends on the armor set you wear – simple, but it changes the whole game.

ClassRoleStrengths
BardSupportBuff aura for the whole squad, keeps the team alive in team fights.
RogueAssassinBurst damage and speed, perfect for cutting enemies off.
MinerEconomyHaste and better yields, cashes out your faction fast.
ArcherRangedDebuffs from range, controls the raid.
Ghoul (maybe)BruiserLifesteal and endurance – the tanky frontline.
CommandWhat it does
/classShows your current class and its passives.
/class listOverview of all classes and effects.
/class info <class>Details of a specific class.

PVP & POTSG

The main game runs on modern 1.21 PvP, but we never forget the classics – from time to time we host PotSG events: 1.8 PvP, smooth pots, honest knockback.

FAQ

When does the server launch?

The estimated launch is September 18, around 6 PM CEST – at least as a beta version.

What is the IP?

We are keeping the IP to ourselves for now. Follow siphcf.eu and you will be the first to know.

No. All rewards are in-game, you cannot buy a combat advantage.

Yes. The difficulty is balanced and the starter kit plus this presentation guide you.

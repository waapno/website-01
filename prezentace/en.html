KINGDOMS

Kingdoms are gone – SIP'S NETWORK runs on KingdomsX. A Kingdom is a group of players who build their own territory together, guard their borders, grow their infrastructure and compete against every other kingdom on the map.

👑 Found

Start a kingdom, pick its name and become its King.

🤝 Recruit

Invite players – members get the permissions of their rank.

🗺️ Expand

Claim chunks, build your capital and push your borders outward.

⚔️ Compete

Make allies, declare enemies, fight for Outposts and invade.

Server rule stays: kingdoms are 3-man squads competing for the Kingdom Top and the payout. Core command is /k.

CommandWhat it does
/k create <name>Founds your kingdom – you become the King.
/k invite <player>Invites a player (max 3 members).
/k acceptAccepts a kingdom invite.
/k leaveLeaves your kingdom.
/k claimClaims the chunk you stand on.
/k unclaimReleases a claimed chunk.
/k mapShows the land map around you.
/k homeTeleports to the kingdom home.
/k sethomeSets the kingdom home.
/k show <kingdom>Kingdom info: members, lands, relations.
/k topKingdom leaderboard – decides the payout.
/k nexusOpens the Nexus – the heart of your kingdom.
/k helpEvery kingdom command.

Commands follow default KingdomsX settings; /kingdoms, /k and /kac work too. Exact limits are set by the server config.

Diplomacy is part of the game. Every pair of kingdoms has a relation that decides who may enter your land, use it and fight inside it.

🕊️ TRUCEA ceasefire – no PvP between you, but no shared land.
⚪ NEUTRALThe default. Your land is protected, PvP follows the land rules.
⚔️ ENEMYOpen war: PvP everywhere and invasions become the goal.
CommandWhat it does

LANDS, CLAIMS & MAP

The world is split into chunks (16×16). Each claimed chunk is a piece of your land: nobody outside your kingdom can build, break or open containers there – unless your relations allow it.

Claiming

Stand in a chunk and use /k claim. Claims usually must connect to your existing land.

Borders

The edge of your land is your front line – defend it with turrets and structures.

Foreign land

On enemy land you are an intruder; enemies can try to take it with an invasion.

Map UI

/k map shows nearby lands – KingdomsX lets you claim directly from the map interface.

Click a chunkEach square is one chunk.

OUTPOSTS

CLAIM THE OUTPOST.
CONTROL THE TERRITORY.

Outposts are a King of the Hill event (/k outposts). Kingdoms fight to keep their members inside a marked zone – the kingdom with the longest control time wins.

  1. A dedicated Outpost area exists on the map.
  2. Kingdoms join the event (an entry fee may apply).
  3. Players fight for control of the zone.
  4. Every second your members hold the zone adds control time.
  5. When time runs out, the kingdom with the most control time wins.
  6. The winner receives the predefined rewards.

💎 Resource Points

Paid straight to the kingdom.

💰 Money

Deposited into the kingdom bank.

🎁 Item rewards

Handed out as physical loot.

Event rules

Time limit, entry fee, min/max participants, PvP inside the zone.

Bossbar

A bossbar shows the event progress live.

Mobs

Mobs may spawn; killing them can give bonus points if configured.

    INVASIONS

    An invasion is war against another kingdom's land – not a zone event. Attackers start an invasion on an enemy chunk; defenders must stop them.

    ⚔️ Attackers

    Pick a target chunk and start the invasion with /k invade. Win and the land is yours.

    🛡️ Defenders

    Rush to your borders and kill the invaders before they succeed.

    🗿 Champion

    Each chunk is guarded by a Champion – a boss the attacker must defeat. Upgrade it in the Nexus.

    🏹 Turrets & Structures

    Turrets shoot intruders, structures like the Powercell strengthen your land.

    Invasions are not Outposts: Outposts = control of a neutral zone; Invasion = taking another kingdom's land.

    TURRETS & STRUCTURES

    Turrets

    Structures

    ⚡ Powercell

    ⛏️ Extractor

    🌀 Warpad

    Fast travel across your land.

    📦 Outpost (structure)

    Buy and sell for resource points – not the Outpost event.

    ⚙️ Regulator

    PROGRESSION

    ✦ Skills XP & Levels

    Skills XP is completely separate from vanilla XP. You earn it mainly by killing mobs (players and stronger mobs give more; spawner mobs much less) and lose a little on death. XP raises your class level (max 100) and new abilities unlock at set levels. By default XP, stats and level are tracked separately for each class.

    Lv 37 → 38

    ◈ Souls

    Souls are the RPG currency. By default every kill gives 1 soul, players 2, the Wither 100 and the Ender Dragon 500. Spend them on abilities (/skills improve), masteries (/skills mastery) and on unlocking classes. Dying costs a few souls (5 by default) – farm carefully.

    ⚔ Stats

    Each level gives you stat points. Invest them in /skills stats. Stats are variables inside ability formulas – in the improve menu every number is coloured by the stat that powers it.

    STRStrength

    Physical power, also adds critical damage (STR / 3).

    INTIntelligence

    Fuels magic-based formulas and energy-related values.

    DEXDexterity

    Agility, also adds critical chance (DEX / 3).

    DEFDefense

    Survivability formulas and shields.

    Try it – crit bonus from your stats (default config)

    Critical damage: +10 · Critical chance: 10 %

    ABILITIES & CONTROLS

    Passive

    Always on. They trigger by themselves while you attack, mine, place blocks or even move.

    Active

    You trigger them with an activation key combo. They cost energy, have a cooldown and some need a specific item in hand.

    Instant Fire immediately, no target needed – e.g. Energy Flux, Asclepius, Blackhole.

    Non-instant After the combo the ability goes into a ready state – then you must hit a target within a few seconds.

    Activation keys

    Press the keys one after another, quickly. Click a combo to see how it is played:

    LLeft-click
    RRight-click
    QDrop item
    SSneak
    C+Sneak + key
    FSwap item
    1. Press combo
    2. Ready state
    3. Hit target
    4. Ability fires

    Signs of the ready state: a purple particle on your right hand, a red action bar and an armor-equip sound. Miss the window and you have to activate again.

    Ability not working? Check that it is unlocked and upgraded, not disabled (grey), you have enough energy, it is not on cooldown and you pressed the keys fast enough.

    Thick SkinChance to take less damage from mobs or players.

      USEFUL TIPS

      Ten quick tips that will save you time, gear and nerves on SIP'S NETWORK.

      [TIP #1]

      Join or found a kingdom as soon as possible – a solo player is an easy target for raiders; a kingdom gives you protected land, turrets and allies.

      [TIP #2]

      Check /k map before you build a base – you will see neighbouring kingdoms and avoid settling right next to an enemy.

      [TIP #3]

      Pick your class with /skill select to match how you play – try it first in the Class Simulator here on this page.

      [TIP #4]

      Upgrade your abilities in /skills improve before jumping into PvP – the level gap in abilities decides fights.

      [TIP #5]

      Spread your Masteries: one gathering, one combat, one utility – you will earn resources and fight better at the same time.

      [TIP #6]

      Watch /boss timer – a world boss is unbeatable solo, but three coordinated players split valuable loot.

      [TIP #7]

      Sell spare loot in /shop and keep an eye on /ah – the best deals on sets and enchants appear there.

      [TIP #8]

      Gamble only what you can afford to lose – the economy is stable, but luck is not a strategy.

      [TIP #9]

      Build turrets and structures on your land – they guard your chests even while you are offline.

      [TIP #10]

      Join our Discord – events, giveaways and payout results are announced there first.
